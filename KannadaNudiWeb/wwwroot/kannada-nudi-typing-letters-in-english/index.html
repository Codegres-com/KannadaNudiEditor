Vowel keys typed alone give the independent vowel. Typed after a consonant they add the vowel sign instead – that is how the kagunita below is formed.

Short and long pairs differ only by Shift: i ಇ / I ಈ, u ಉ / U ಊ, e ಎ / E ಏ, o ಒ / O ಓ.

Frequently asked questions

How do I download the Nudi letters PDF in English?

Click “Download as PDF / Print” at the top and choose “Save as PDF” in the print dialog.

What is the English key for ಳ in Nudi?

Upper-case L. Lower-case l is ಲ.

Which English key is ಡ in Nudi?

w is ಡ and W is ಢ. The retroflex ಟ / ಠ are on q / Q.

How do I type ಅಂ and ಅಃ?

aM gives ಅಂ and aH gives ಅಃ. After a consonant, M and H add ಂ and ಃ.
