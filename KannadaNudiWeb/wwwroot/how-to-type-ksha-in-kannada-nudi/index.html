How to Type ಕ್ಷ (Ksha) in Kannada Nudi

ನುಡಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಕ್ಷ, ಜ್ಞ, ಶ್ರೀ ಟೈಪ್ ಮಾಡುವುದು ಹೇಗೆ?

ಕ್ಷ is not a separate key in Nudi – it is ಕ + halant + ಷ. Press kfx: k for ಕ, f to join, and x for ಷ.

ಶ್ರೀ, ತ್ರ, ಸ್ತ್ರೀ – ra as an ottakshara

Visible halant: f vs F

Lower-case f joins the two consonants into a conjunct (ಕ್ಕ). Upper-case F keeps the halant visible and does not form a conjunct – useful for English words and names written in Kannada. At the end of a word, f alone gives the halant: bsf → ಬಸ್.

Frequently asked questions

How do I type ksha (ಕ್ಷ) in Nudi?

Press k f x. k is ಕ, f is the halant that joins consonants, and x is ಷ.

Which key is ಷ in Nudi?

Lower-case x is ಷ. Upper-case S is ಶ and lower-case s is ಸ.

How do I type ಶ್ರೀ in Nudi?

Press S f r I.

How do I type ಜ್ಞಾನ in Nudi?

Press j f z A n.

How do I type ಲಕ್ಷ್ಮಿ in Nudi?

Press l k f x f m i – two f presses for the double ottakshara.
