Speak and let the editor type for you. Kannada Nudi Editor includes Kannada and English voice typing, so you can dictate letters, notes and articles instead of typing them.

Start typing in Kannada – it's free

How to use voice typing

  1. Open the editor in an up-to-date desktop or mobile browser.
  2. Pick Kannada or English next to the speech button.
  3. Click Start Speech and allow microphone access.
  4. Speak naturally. Text appears at the cursor after each pause. Click Stop Speech when done.

Runs on your device

Speech recognition uses an on-device Whisper model that runs inside your browser. Your voice is processed locally and is never sent to a server. The model downloads once the first time you use it and is cached for next time.

Tips for better accuracy

  • Use a headset or speak close to the microphone in a quiet room.
  • Pause briefly between sentences – each pause sends a segment for recognition.
  • Correct any mistakes with the Nudi or Baraha keyboard without leaving the editor.

Frequently asked questions

Is Kannada voice typing free?

Yes. It is free with no time limits or sign-up.

Is my voice recorded or uploaded?

No. Recognition runs locally in your browser; audio does not leave your device.

Can I dictate in English too?

Yes. Switch the speech language to English to dictate English text.

Why does it take a moment the first time?

The speech model is downloaded once on first use. After that it loads from your browser's cache.

Ready to write in Kannada?

The editor runs entirely in your browser – no sign-up, no installation.
