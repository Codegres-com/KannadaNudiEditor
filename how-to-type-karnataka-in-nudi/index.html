How to Type Karnataka (ಕರ್ನಾಟಕ) in Nudi

ನುಡಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ‘ಕರ್ನಾಟಕ’ ಎಂದು ಬರೆಯುವುದು ಹೇಗೆ?

To write ಕರ್ನಾಟಕ in Nudi, press krfnAqk. The only tricky part is ರ್ನಾ: you type the ರ first and join it with f, and Nudi draws it as the arkavattu (ರ್ hook) after ನಾ.

Why is ರ typed before ನ?

Nudi follows the spoken order of sounds: kar-naa-ṭa-ka. The ರ್ is pronounced before ನಾ, so you type it first. Kannada script then writes this “half ra” as the arkavattu – the small hook drawn after the next letter – which is why it appears to the right of ನಾ on screen. The same rule gives ಧರ್ಮ, ಸೂರ್ಯ and ಕಾರ್ಯ.

Frequently asked questions

How do I type Karnataka in Kannada Nudi?

Press k r f n A q k. The f joins ರ to ನ, which Kannada writes as the arkavattu.

How do I write Karnataka in Nudi with the arkavattu?

Type the ರ before the letter it sits on and press f after it: ರ + f + ನಾ. You never type the arkavattu hook separately.

Why do I get ಕರ್ನಾತಕ instead of ಕರ್ನಾಟಕ?

You pressed t (ತ). The retroflex ಟ is on q.

How do I type ಕರ್ನಾಟಕ on my phone?

Install Kannada Nudi Keyboard for Android or the iPhone app, choose the Nudi layout and use the same keys.
