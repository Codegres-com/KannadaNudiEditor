Nudi 6.0 to Nudi 4.0 Converter

Need Kannada in the old Nudi 4.0 format – for DTP software, a printing press or an office template that only works with Nudi fonts? Paste Unicode Kannada typed in Nudi 6.0 / 6.1 (or anywhere else) and get Nudi ASCII text to use with the Nudi 01 e family of fonts.

How to use the converted Nudi 4.0 text

  1. Paste or type Unicode Kannada in the left box (from Nudi 6.x, this editor, a website or WhatsApp).
  2. Click Copy under the right box.
  3. Paste into Word, PageMaker, CorelDRAW or Photoshop.
  4. Select the pasted text and set the font to Nudi 01 e (or another Nudi ASCII font). It will display as Kannada.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert Nudi 6.0 text to Nudi 4.0?

Paste the Unicode Kannada into the left box on this page, copy the result on the right, paste it into your document and apply a Nudi ASCII font such as Nudi 01 e.

Why does the output look like English letters?

Nudi 4.0 stores Kannada as Latin characters and needs a Nudi font to display them. Apply Nudi 01 e (or similar) to the pasted text.

Can I convert back to Unicode later?

Yes. Use the Nudi ASCII to Unicode converter.

Does it convert Kannada numerals?

Yes. Kannada digits (೧೨೩) become 1 2 3, which Nudi fonts draw as Kannada numerals.

Is my text uploaded?

No. The conversion runs in your browser.
