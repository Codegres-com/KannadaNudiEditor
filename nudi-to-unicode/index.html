Why convert Nudi ASCII to Unicode?

Older Nudi and Baraha fonts store Kannada as English letters drawn with a special font. Without that exact font installed the text looks like PÀ£ÀßqÀ instead of ಕನ್ನಡ, it cannot be searched, and it breaks when pasted into email, phones or websites. Unicode fixes all of that.

How to convert a file

  1. Open the Kannada Nudi Editor.
  2. Click Open and choose your .docx or .txt file.
  3. When asked how to read the file, choose ASCII (Nudi/Baraha) and click Import.
  4. The document opens as Unicode Kannada. Check it, then click Save to download the converted .docx.

For Word documents, paragraphs, headings, bold/italic and tables are kept – only the Kannada text is converted.

Private by design

The conversion runs entirely in your browser using WebAssembly. Your documents are never uploaded to a server, which makes it safe for office and government files.

Frequently asked questions

Which file types can I convert?

Microsoft Word (.docx) and plain text (.txt) files. For older .doc files, open them in Word and save as .docx first.

My file is already readable Kannada. Which option do I pick?

Choose Unicode (Standard) on import – the text is already Unicode and needs no conversion.

Does it handle ottaksharas and vattaksharas correctly?

Yes. The converter maps Nudi glyph sequences, including conjuncts and vowel signs, to the correct Unicode characters.

Is the converter free?

Yes, it is free with no file limits.
