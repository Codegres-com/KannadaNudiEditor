Frequently asked questions

Is there a Kannada Nudi keyboard chart PDF?

Yes – click “Download as PDF / Print” on this page and choose “Save as PDF”. The print layout contains just the keyboard diagram and letter charts.

Is this chart the same as Nudi 6.1 and KGP?

Yes. It follows the Nudi / KGP layout standardised by the Government of Karnataka and KAGAPA, used by Nudi 4.0, 5.0, 6.0 and 6.1.

Which key is the halant (ottu) in Nudi?

f. Type it between two consonants to join them, e.g. kfk gives ಕ್ಕ.

Which key is ಝ in Nudi?

Upper-case J. Lower-case j is ಜ, so JA gives ಝಾ.
